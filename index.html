Welcome to Willowbend. Pick a tile and make it yours.

A little less setup. A little more making.LOCAL FIRST · OPEN SOURCE · MIT LICENSE

Tab through the palette and tools. On the map, use arrow keys to move and Space or Enter to paint. For a rectangle, Space sets the first corner and Space again sets the second. Escape cancels a stroke. B brush, E erase, F fill, R rectangle, I eyedropper, G grid. Ctrl or Command Z undoes; Shift Z redoes.
