Field guide & controls

Move with the arrow keys or WASD. On touch screens, swipe the map or use the direction pad. Space waits; P pauses; R restarts.

Wardens move after you. Their red outlines mark the next step.

Dash moves two tiles before patrols move. Walking and waiting recharge it.

Crates hold copper plates down. Leave them in place to keep bridges open.

Interact · E talks to keepers, rings nearby relays, or opens supplies.

Decoy · Q throws a false lantern ahead. Shield · C protects this turn and the next two.

Fern cover hides quiet movement from distant hunters. Dashes are audible for three turns.

Watcher sweeps strike the crossed cells next turn. Leave the lane or shield before it hits.

Embers pulse each turn. Orange outlines warn where they will burn next.

Singing relics attract hunters while carried. X sets one down as a false trail; E picks it up again. Deliver it to the beacon.

Plan · V lists nearby enemy steps, marked sweeps, and next-turn ember cells without spending a turn.

Valves change crossings and ember fields. Their preview explains what changes; occupied crossings cannot close.

Nearby action chooses between adjacent keepers, valves, relays, relics, and supplies. E uses your selected action.

Trades spend unspent coins once per keeper. Collected coins still count toward treasure goals.

Expeditions carry supplies forward. Camp restores a heart and offers an upgrade and two routes. Checkpoints and exported backups let you continue later.

Three stars reward a clean route within the trail’s target turns. Coins add bonus points.

A little less setup. A little more making.LOCAL FIRST · OPEN SOURCE · MIT LICENSE

Tab through the palette and tools. On the map, use arrow keys to move and Space or Enter to paint. For a rectangle, Space sets the first corner and Space again sets the second. Escape cancels a stroke. B brush, E erase, F fill, R rectangle, I eyedropper, H pan, G grid. With Pan selected, drag to scroll or use arrow keys. Use Fit map to see the entire map. Ctrl or Command Z undoes; Shift Z redoes.
