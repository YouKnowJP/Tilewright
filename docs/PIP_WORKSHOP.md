# Pip’s invention workshop

Open **Adventure Workshop → Puzzle Lab → Visit Pip’s workshop**. The same
inventions are available from the Trailhouse village’s **Your invention display**.
Pip’s program panel also offers a short practice lesson based on the station
or obstacle Pip is currently facing. Practice leaves your adventure untouched.

## Ten short inventions

| Collection | Adventures | Idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learn with Pip | A parcel for Tavi; A corner of sky; The jade invitation; A sign that remembers | Use, turning programs, parcel recipes and remembered routes |
| Tinkerer’s Trials | Post above the clouds; The jade express; Rook’s wrong turn; The quiet garden | Solve with a limited parts tray; discover a different answer |
| Pip & Sprout | Pip meets Sprout; Two hands, one bridge | Optional two-companion prototypes: handoffs and shared plate timing |

All challenges are available immediately. The time shown is only an estimate;
there is no deadline. Hints progress from a nudge to an explanation. The worked
example replaces only that challenge’s draft after a confirmation. You can Undo
that edit and continue inventing your own solution.

## Build and test

Set three cards for each companion. Pick a part, configure it, then tap a free
garden tile. Choose **Inspect tile** to read a station without changing it.
Arrow keys move the yellow cursor; Space places or inspects. The folded garden text view also offers Column/Row selectors, Go to tile and Place selected part for precise phone input. A text garden list
includes every station’s coordinates. Parcel numbers identify colours too.
To revise a placed part, select its tile with **Inspect tile** or the arrow keys,
then choose **Edit this part**. Its actual prism operation, recipe or two route
programs load into the tray. Change the settings and choose **Apply settings to
this part**. The edit replaces that tile’s part within the same budget. The
first-visit choice lets a route memory start with A or B. **Remove this part**
and **Undo edit** make it easy to try a different idea. Fixed stations remain
part of the challenge and cannot be edited.

In the ten classic inventions, parts cannot replace fixed stations, starting positions or water. Tubes need
both ends. Each tray limits both total parts and the available kind of part.

**Test invention** starts a fresh simulation. **One tick** advances every
companion once; **Next event** stops at the next Use or obstacle. **Run** continues
until completion, a repeating state, or the 240-tick safety limit. Pause, Undo a
tick, Reset the test, or return to the bench. Closing the workshop, changing
sections or hiding the browser tab stops playback. Your ordinary trail does not
advance during practice.

In relay prototypes, **Pip acts first and Sprout second**. They cannot overlap or
swap through each other. Pip can Use the H handoff when an empty-handed partner
is beside it. The receiver may accept the parcel later in that same tick. A
held or occupied bridge stays safe for the whole tick. A blocked move still
advances its program card. Relay prototypes stay in this workshop; ordinary
adventures continue to use a single Pip.

## Pip & Sprout: six story missions and your own adventures

Choose **Pip & Sprout · story & creator** in Puzzle Lab, or the **Pip & Sprout · 6 story missions** collection at the bench. All six chapters are available from the start:

| Chapter | Story | New idea | Worked clear |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A Letter for Two | Timed handoff and remembered departure | 9 ticks |
| 2 | The Dyehouse Promise | Two recipes, one parcel | 11 ticks |
| 3 | A Bridge for the Rain | Colour-linked crossings and tick-start power | 13 ticks |
| 4 | The Long Way Round | Paired courier tubes and Sprout’s home flag | 19 ticks |
| 5 | The Signmaker’s Errand | Two deliveries, return loops and a first B route | 27 ticks |
| 6 | The Festival of Two Lights | Signal stamps, two windows and both partners home | 55 ticks |

Use **Pip’s cards / Sprout’s cards** to focus on one program. Story gardens are fixed; **Remix this garden** copies all stations, dialogue, cards and credit into an editable adventure. The original remains available. **Next chapter** appears after a story clear. Playback pace offers Steady, Quick or One moment at a time; manual ticks remain available.

In **Create a duo adventure**, choose road/water brushes or move either starting companion. Parts may occupy starting tiles in duo adventures. Remove a station and move any companion before painting its tile as water. Gardens range from 7–13 columns and 5–9 rows; blank presets include 9×7, 11×9 and 13×9. Keep at most 32 parts, eight memories/signals, one home flag per companion and two tube ends per colour.

Every window needs one to three matching parcels. Optional handoff requirements (zero to four) and P/S home flags add goals. Matching bridge colours operate independently. Power is sampled at the start of the tick; a matching occupied bridge keeps that crossing safe for the tick. **Use on M** samples matching held plates or completed windows: all matching plates held **or** all matching windows full selects Powered; otherwise Quiet. Either bank restarts at card 1 and keeps cargo. Signals with no matching hardware stay Quiet. Pip acts first, so a Sprout delivery later in the tick affects Pip’s next sample.

The folded **Your story & goals** drawer edits opening/completion dialogue. **Your adventure shelf** keeps twelve named maps; Save updates a loaded entry after confirmation, Save a new copy uses an empty slot, and a full shelf never evicts anything. Opening another custom map confirms before replacing a different bench draft. Export files or full journey backups for safekeeping. Shelf and blueprint storage are local to the current journey.

### Customizable duo blueprints

The folded **Blueprints** drawer contains a recipe atelier, a two-route sign, a tube pair and a linked crossing. Configure input/output parcels, required deliveries, link colour, first route and facing. Rotation turns geometry, direction cards and mirror orientation together. Saved/imported pieces can shift all four colours together, preserving their internal links. Previews include a text description.

Select Inspect, put the garden cursor at the desired top-left corner, then **Place at cursor**. The whole piece must fit and its parts must avoid existing parts. Roads merge safely; companions stay in place. A placement is one Undo edit and clears an old mechanical proof. **Capture & keep blueprint** takes a chosen rectangle from the cursor, excluding companions; a tube pair must be captured together. Keep up to twelve pieces, download JSON to share a reusable piece or open a friend’s file for preview. A full shelf refuses additions without removing anything.

Duo adventures use project format 2 inside the existing PT1 file/code/link container. Working clears are replayed before being labelled verified. Duo projects play here; ordinary single-Pip adventure exports retain their current format. No server, account or automatic upload is involved.

## Keep something worth returning to

Completion records your fastest, smallest and latest distinct answers, up to
three per challenge. Optimization is optional and unlocks nothing. **Dress Pip**
offers four paints, three antennae and three ribbon choices without grinding.
Sprout keeps a fern body and leaf antenna to distinguish the partners. Pip’s
expressions reflect its current task; movement effects honor existing settings.
Successful parcel stations use the existing optional sound/volume controls.

**Your inventions** holds three working projects. Displaying a new one in an
occupied slot asks before replacement. Reopen a display to replay or remix it.
Drafts, bests, displays and looks are local to your selected save journey and
included in full journey backups. Fresh start clears them for that journey.
Unreadable existing storage is preserved; failed writes keep the current visit
playable. Download project files to keep work when saving is unavailable.

## Share and discover

Download a `.json` Pip project, copy its **PT1** code, or copy a `#pip=` play link
from a hosted copy. A file or code works from a local development copy too.
The import verifies a solution by replaying it before describing it as working.
Changes to parts or cards remove a stale solution. Renaming an invention or its
creator at the bench keeps a working clear, and Undo restores the visible
name fields along with the saved project. Drafts can be shared honestly as unfinished.

A finished single-companion invention can also download as an ordinary
Tilewright adventure. Its creator clear is checked again under the main game
rules before export. Import it in the main Workshop to remix a longer adventure
or use the existing adventure sharing tools. Relay prototypes share as Pip
projects. **Download postcard** creates a board image and invention statistics.

**Player adventures → Community shelf** includes four original, official builder
picks and the player-made worlds saved on this device. Filter by invention type
or challenge. These are not public uploads, ratings or online search results.
Share directly with friends, or manually suggest your own work for owner review
through the linked GitHub form. GitHub submissions are public and need an account;
playing shared files and links does not.

## Better pacing on ordinary trails

Open Pip’s program panel and expand **Advance to the next event**. Choose the
next station/event or delivery/obstacle. This spends normal turns while your
keeper stays still. Enemies and hazards still act. It stops for blocked moves,
repeating routes, injury/alarm, dialogue, completion or its 120-turn limit.
Any key, board tap, menu interaction or **Stop advancing** interrupts it. Each
turn retains normal Undo and checkpoint behavior; Pip’s loop setting is separate.

The free rehearsal offers 6, 12 or 24 cards, with a numbered route and written
results. It starts the draft at card 1, changes no game state and does not model
future keeper movement or enemies. Its existing bridge/plate conditions can
change once you walk. Contextual advice explains the actual parcel recipe,
receiver, next remembered route or blocked card.

## Feedback

**Share & feedback → Help make the next invention better** downloads a short
local feedback note. Nothing is uploaded. It contains the challenge, solution
metrics, your chosen reaction and up to 400 characters of comments. No journey
backup or identity is included. Share the note manually if you choose. Browser
automation verifies behavior; it does not replace observing volunteer players.
