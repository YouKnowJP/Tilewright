# Pip’s invention workshop

Choose **Play Pip & Sprout** on the main menu for Adventure Home. Browse all
forty-four duo chapters in six-card pages; filter by region, saved/completed
progress or title/mechanic words. The suggested road follows verified story
clears, while every chapter remains available. Continue restores an ordinary
story's saved cards/parts, or a collection chapter's exact test and Undo history.
**Your collections**, **Create & share** and **How to play** provide direct
routes to saved collections, the creator, builder, imports and classic lessons.

The workbench's Garden/Cards/Tools/Hints shortcuts keep navigation close. Tools
is offered for repairs and creator gardens. The full briefing/progress and
advanced adventure chooser stay available below the compact play area.
Test/Undo/playback sit beside the garden. Staged duo hints reveal an authored
clue, an experiment and finally the exact optional worked route. Previous clue
does not edit the adventure. Reading hints pauses automatic playback.

Three tiny practice gardens use the real engine in separate session state:
handoffs, next-tick bell power and passport cargo checks. Choose one next card
for each friend, then advance both; Undo/reset or follow a guided run. They
teach individual actions before three-card route planning. Closing practice
restores focus to the adventure; no practice proof, best or save is awarded.

Open **Adventure Workshop → Puzzle Lab → Visit Pip’s workshop**. The same
inventions are available from the Trailhouse village’s **Your invention display**.
Pip’s program panel also offers a short practice lesson based on the station
or obstacle Pip is currently facing. Practice leaves your adventure untouched.

## Build and test

Set three cards for each companion. Pick a part, configure it, then tap a free
garden tile. Choose **Inspect tile** to read a station without changing it.
Arrow keys move the yellow cursor; Space places or inspects. The folded garden text view also offers Column/Row selectors, Go to tile and Place selected part for precise phone input. A text garden list
includes every station’s coordinates. Parcel numbers identify colours too.
To revise a placed part, select its tile with **Inspect tile** or the arrow keys,
then choose **Edit this part**. Its actual prism operation, recipe or two route
programs load into the tray. Change the settings and choose **Apply settings to
this part**. The edit replaces that tile’s part within the same budget. The
first-visit choice lets a route memory start with A or B. **Remove this part**
and **Undo edit** make it easy to try a different idea. Fixed stations remain
part of the challenge and cannot be edited.

In the ten classic inventions, parts cannot replace fixed stations, starting positions or water. Tubes need
both ends. Each tray limits both total parts and the available kind of part.

**Test invention** starts a fresh simulation. **One tick** advances every
companion once; **Next event** stops at the next Use or obstacle. **Run** continues
until completion, a repeating state, or the 240-tick safety limit. Pause, Undo a
tick, Reset the test, or return to the bench. Closing the workshop, changing
sections, leaving the game window or hiding the browser tab stops playback.
Choosing other workshop controls pauses it too. The playback line says when the
test is paused; returning never restarts it. Your ordinary trail does not advance
during practice.

Focus **Garden** to use keyboard controls. Arrows or your saved WASD/IJKL layout
inspect tiles; your inspection repeat preference applies. At the bench,
Space/Enter places or inspects the selected part. During a test, Space/Enter
advances one tick and R runs/pauses. Z undoes an edit or test tick. Holding an
action key never repeats it. Escape first pauses a running test; press again to
leave. Controller Start runs/pauses, Back pauses before leaving, and A advances
when the garden has focus. Menus and confirmations own their controller input.
Practice's focused garden supports Space/Enter or controller A for the next pair
of cards, and Z/controller Undo for one tick. Text fields and browser shortcuts
keep their normal behavior. See [controls](CONTROLS.md).

In relay prototypes, **Pip acts first and Sprout second**. They cannot overlap or
swap through each other. Pip can Use the H handoff when an empty-handed partner
is beside it. The receiver may accept the parcel later in that same tick. A
held or occupied bridge stays safe for the whole tick. A blocked move still
advances its program card. Relay prototypes stay in this workshop; ordinary
adventures continue to use a single Pip.

### Reconnect a blueprint

Open **Blueprints → Reconnect tubes & lockers** when the selected piece has those parts. Each row maps one original numbered connection to a destination number. Both tube ends or locker ends move together. Tube and locker numbers are independent, so changing a locker never changes a tube, parcel well, recipe, window, signal or cargo condition.

Separate networks inside a piece need separate destinations. When swapping two numbers, the preview may report a duplicate until both destinations are selected; placement stays disabled until the complete map is valid. **Restore piece connections** returns the numbers to the current piece’s design.

**Use unused connections** reserves numbers not used by that kind of station anywhere in the garden. It keeps already-free numbers when possible. If there are too few numbers, it reports the shortage and makes no partial change. Manual choices can intentionally connect a one-ended locker blueprint to a locker already in the garden. The resulting placement still obeys the two-end limit per connection.

Rewiring changes the preview until a piece is placed, kept, updated or downloaded. Saved and shared files contain the resulting ordinary station numbers; no new file format is required. Choosing another piece resets its connection controls. These tools remain folded and are locked during a test.

## Better pacing on ordinary trails

Open Pip’s program panel and expand **Advance to the next event**. Choose the
next station/event or delivery/obstacle. This spends normal turns while your
keeper stays still. Enemies and hazards still act. It stops for blocked moves,
repeating routes, injury/alarm, dialogue, completion or its 120-turn limit.
Any key, board tap, menu interaction or **Stop advancing** interrupts it. Each
turn retains normal Undo and checkpoint behavior; Pip’s loop setting is separate.

The free rehearsal offers 6, 12 or 24 cards, with a numbered route and written
results. It starts the draft at card 1, changes no game state and does not model
future keeper movement or enemies. Its existing bridge/plate conditions can
change once you walk. Contextual advice explains the actual parcel recipe,
receiver, next remembered route or blocked card.
