# Your traveler and the Lanternfair

![The Traveler’s Tent](screenshots/traveler-tent-desktop.png)

Choose **Your traveler** on the title screen, or **Options → Your traveler**
during a trail. Back returns to the place you opened it. Changing clothes,
reading, and turning the preview never spend a game turn.

## A look for every road

Six ready-made outfits offer woodland, garden, coast, starlight, sunset, and
clockwork looks. Presets change the coat, scarf, headwear, and lantern while
keeping your name, skin tone, and hair choices.

Open **Tailor your own look** for a name of up to 24 characters, six coat
colors, six scarf colors, six skin tones, six hair colors, three hairstyles,
four headwear choices, and four lantern colors. All choices are available from
the beginning. Appearance changes are cosmetic and give no puzzle advantage.

**Turn preview** shows the front, sides, and back. **Surprise outfit** mixes a
look while keeping your name, skin tone, and camp settings. **Original look**
restores the original appearance and atmosphere while keeping your name.

The same traveler appears in adventures, training, creator playtests, and route
reviews. Clothing and your name are saved with this browser journey and included
in its complete game backup. Other journeys have their own traveler. If browser
saving is unavailable, the options explain that the look lasts for this visit.

## Light and atmosphere

Choose **Forest dusk**, **Rose dawn**, or **Starfall camp** for the title-screen
scenery. Your lantern can have a soft, steady halo or show just its lamp. High
contrast removes the halo. These choices preserve hazard colors, map symbols,
and readable interface panels; no flashing or continuous motion is introduced.

## The Lanternfair Letters

Choose **Find the Lanternfair story** in the tent, or its cover in
**Adventure Workshop → Storybooks**. The four chapters can also be played,
bookmarked, and remixed individually from **Player worlds → Discover → Peaceful**.

| Chapter | Main task | Optional treasure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Buttonmaker’s Shortcut | Find the spare key, unlock the pattern-house crossing, and restore two lights. | Three scattered buttons. |
| A River of Ribbon | Push a crate onto its plate and leave the bridge open for the return. | Three ribbon spools. |
| The Bellmaker’s Fitting Room | Ring Road (1), Rain (2), Home (3), then explore the far garden. | Three threads. |
| The Lanternfair Parade | Keep a bridge open, restore both lamps, invite Iris home, and deliver the singing heart. | A flower, shell, and star. |

The worlds have no enemies or burning tiles and can be completed by walking and
interacting. Bell order and mechanism links are written and numbered, so color
recognition is unnecessary. Quest keeps clues available, and Undo can repair a
misplaced push. Optional camp promises invite treasure, guide, clean-route, and
turn-target runs. Missing an optional promise never blocks the ending.

The four maps are ordinary portable worlds. **Make your version** copies the
book for editing; share the result through the existing file, code, or play-link
tools. See [Storybooks](STORYBOOKS.md), [saved-data help](SAVED_DATA.md), and
[accessibility options](ACCESSIBILITY.md).
