# Try another answer; keep your place

**Undo · Z** sits below the board beside Controls. Use it to revisit up to
**60 recent meaningful actions**: movement, tools, interactions, dialogue
choices, and forgetting learned habits. A free conversation close folds into
the preceding action, so Undo lets you reconsider the choice itself.

The whole previous state returns: health, supplies, crates, mechanisms, fog,
companions, carried cargo, specialist tools, and hunter memory. Undo spends no
turn. A lost attempt offers **Undo last action** on its result screen. Completed
wins, awarded records, and expedition camp transitions cannot be undone.
There is no gameplay Redo button; make the same input again or try a different
answer. Painting Undo/Redo in the editors remain independent.

Choose the visible button, press **Z**, or click the right stick on an enabled
standard controller. Undo is also in Menu and ordinary conversations. Holding
the key or controller button does not repeatedly undo. Options and unrelated
dialogs block it; typing shortcuts still belong to their text fields.

## Return after closing the page

The game stores one **latest adventure** on this browser and device. Reloading
opens the title; choose **Continue trail** deliberately to restore its exact
position and recent Undo history. Story missions, Guild jobs, player worlds,
Puzzle Lab, Storybook chapters, Trailhouse commissions/festival, custom terrain
adventures, and expeditions are supported. Starting another adventure replaces
this latest slot; browsing menus or reading guides does not.

For the active Storybook's saved chapter, **Resume chapter** returns to that
same run. If another adventure has replaced it, **Restart chapter** retains
completed chapter receipts and the selected promise, then begins a fresh
attempt. Replacing the active book asks first and retires only that old book's
Continue slot. Each new chapter still starts with fresh supplies and habits.

The two training lessons each keep separate saved practice: lesson progress,
construction history, unfinished story text, and playtest actions. Select the
lesson again to return. **Start this lesson again** begins a fresh practice.
These saves never replace the main adventure or your Workshop draft.

## Save status and boundaries

**Saved here** means the latest checkpoint was written and read back successfully.
**Session only** means the latest moves could not be stored. Keep the tab open;
gameplay and recent Undo still work. A previous checkpoint may remain on disk,
so reloading after a failed write may return to an earlier turn. Corrupt or
incompatible checkpoints show a message; completed records and worlds remain
separate. Completing an ordinary adventure removes its unfinished checkpoint.

The universal checkpoint is capped at **650 KB** and an ordinary attempt at
**8,192 recorded inputs**. Extremely large/long attempts can keep playing with
session-only recovery. Recent Undo keeps only 60 state frames; it is rebuilt
from the verified action journal after reload. Training has its own 300 KB
practice limit and usual 2,000-action creator-clear limit. Expedition backups
retain their existing limits and portable export/import.

These are local browser saves. A play link does not transfer or synchronize
progress. Download worlds/books, expedition checkpoints, and the relevant
mode journals before changing devices or clearing browser data. There is no
portable export for the universal latest-trail slot in this release.
