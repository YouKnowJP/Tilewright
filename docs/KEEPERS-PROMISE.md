# More roads through Lantern Trails — v0.25

## Follow a promise

Open **Adventure Workshop → Storybooks → The Keeper’s Promise**. Restore the
crossroads light, then decide which letter to carry at camp. Moss’s letter
leads to a permanent ferry crossing and a river rescue. Ilex’s letter leads
through a key-and-bell workshop to Vesper’s listening garden. Each route plays
three of the book’s five worlds and has its own ending. Both rules are available;
chapter honors stay optional. Replay the book to see the other road.

![Choose which promise to carry](keepers-promise-choice.png)

Choices and completed chapters save at camp. Continue also retains the exact
unfinished chapter. Finished pages remain readable in the book’s restored pages.

## Write branching adventures

Bind or remix a Storybook. Under each world, expand **Road after this chapter**.
Choose the ordinary next world, jump forward, offer two player choices, or finish
with an ending. Write what each decision changes and choose its destination.
Routes can rejoin or lead to separate endings. Links always go forward, so a book
cannot trap players in a loop. The first world must lead to another world.

A book holds two to six world snapshots. Changing a linked book’s order requires
setting its roads back to **In order** first, keeping destinations from silently
changing. Branching files use Storybook format v2; existing v1 books and saves
remain supported. Download the book and playtest every route before sharing it.

## Build an Expedition toolkit

Choose a **Trail specialist** before starting a journey or single contract.
Camp offers include matching relics while any remain. Each specialist has two:

| Specialist | Relics | A build to try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bellrunner | Lingering chorus; Resonant buckle | Longer dazes plus a two-turn ward, with a longer recharge and three-energy cast. |
| Mistweaver | Long dusk ribbon; Moon thread | Longer veil with a quicker recharge, balanced by shorter ordinary decoys. Echo lantern can improve those decoys. |
| Echoist | Memory anchor; Homeward afterglow | A longer-lived double plus a protected return, balanced by return cost and casting recharge. |

Plan describes the current costs and timers. Supplies carry between legs. Relics
must match the specialist. Daily trails use the same Wayfarer kit for everyone;
custom specialist choices return when leaving daily mode. Existing expeditions
retain their classic rules and checkpoint behavior.

## Meet Vesper

Vesper, the Listener, wears a lilac coat and gold listening fins. Repeated dashes
can teach it a marked long reach; predictable directions can teach it a farther
cutoff. It learns from successful recorded movement, rather than future input.
Plan shows its current tactic, committed move, and any marked sweep before
the player acts. Quiet cover, mist, false lights, and recovery turns can interrupt
its pursuit. The southern garden offers a quieter route past optional treasures.

Creators can select a hunter and choose the Listener personality in its object
notes, then edit its name, sight distance, and route.

## Keep reusable puzzle assemblies

Open **World studio → Puzzle assemblies**. Capture a region by dragging on the
map, choosing two corners with the keyboard, or entering its bounds. Name it,
then save it. Move the map cursor, reopen assemblies, choose a rotation, and
place a copy. One Undo restores the entire placement; Redo reapplies it.

Keep the start and beacon outside the selection. Include every waypoint and all
objects on a linked channel. Assemblies hold up to 12 × 12 cells; twelve personal
slots are available. Placement needs an area without existing objects and enough
unused channels. Terrain in that rectangle is replaced. Copies receive fresh
IDs, independent channels, and shifted patrol/waystone coordinates. Waystones
retain their toolkit when placed in a world without one. Copied bells append to
the world’s existing global melody. Check and playtest the combined world.

Assemblies are part of the current named journey and its full backup. Sharing an
adventure includes the placed puzzles; personal assembly shelves stay local.

## Discover, remember, and review

In **Player adventures → Show**, choose **Trailboard · discover**. Browse the
curated catalogue, filter by story/puzzle/stealth, bookmark a world, and open its
preview to verify or watch its creator clear. **My bookmarks** also includes
bookmarked worlds from your shelf. The catalogue ships with the game. Open
publishing, accounts, ratings, and automatic community uploads are future work.
Players can continue exchanging worlds through files, codes, and public play links.

After finishing or losing a trail, choose **Review route**. Scrub recorded
actions, jump to important moments, and compare the last attempt with the fastest
recorded finish of that adventure, rule set, and specialist. **Adventure journal →
Review a recorded route** opens recent reviews after reload. Reviews replay the
original hunter memory, tools, and discovered fog. They never advance the live
run or award another reward. Four recent adventures are retained within 2 MB;
oversized or unavailable review storage never blocks ordinary progress.

Complete a promise road, then visit **Trailhouse → Your village**. Read the new
scene, choose an earned ferry or paper-star decoration, and ask restored keepers
to remember your journey. Real rescues can also add memories; practice and review
replays do not. Memories and decorations travel in full journey backups, while
the existing Trailhouse passport continues to carry its own village progression.
