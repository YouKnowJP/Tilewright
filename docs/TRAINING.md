# A first trail, then a world of your own

Open [Lantern Trails](https://youknowjp.github.io/Personal-Project/) and choose
**Training camp · Start here**. Both lessons are available immediately, can be
replayed, and have no enemies or time pressure. Lessons and Main menu stay at
the top of the camp. Leaving for the menu preserves your ordinary open trail;
Continue returns to it unchanged.

## First Light Crossing

The coach shows one objective at a time. A bright dashed square marks the next
place to investigate; **Show me a hint** gives more precise directions.

Walk with arrows, WASD, swipes, or the direction pad. Stand beside Mira and
choose **Interact** (E). Read her advice, open the river door using its lever,
walk onto the golden lantern, return to Mira, and Interact again. Lead her to
the turquoise beacon to finish. Talking and Plan pause the world, and blocked
steps cost no turns. Wait is available to practise turn controls.

This is an actual Lantern Trails level using the normal game engine, with
fresh local opponent memory. The lesson records completion after a real win.
It does not add campaign stars or change learned habits or an expedition.

## A little island, made by you

The guided practice table marks three building spots. Choose a brush and tap
its mark, or use arrows and Space. Paint the river crossing with Bridge, then
place a Lantern and a Keeper. Undo and Redo let you revisit an edit.

Open **Your story** to name the world, write an opening, and give the keeper
a useful hint. **Save story**, then **Check world**. Missing goals and broken
routes are checked using the full Workshop's real validator. Once those steps
are complete, **Playtest** starts the world with normal movement and interaction.
Restore its lantern and walk onto its beacon. This win records your actual
action journal as a replayable creator clear, up to the usual 2,000-action limit.

Then choose one of these ways to keep your work:

- **Download my world:** a portable `.trail.json` adventure with your story and
  attached clear. Another player opens Workshop → Import & play to choose it.
- **Copy play link:** available on the public website for smaller worlds.
  The recipient opens a preview and can play directly. If clipboard access is
  unavailable, copy the selected link manually or use the file instead.
- **Keep building in Workshop:** copy your practice world into the full
  designer, where brushes can be used anywhere. If there is an existing draft,
  cancelling the replacement prompt keeps that draft and the lesson intact.

**Return to building** reopens the practice table. Editing invalidates its old
checks and creator clear; check and win the changed version before sharing it.

## What stays saved

Lesson completion is stored under `tilewright.training.v1`. If browser storage
is unavailable, the lessons still work for the visit and the camp reports the
limitation. Practice drafts themselves last for the current visit. Download
your world or take it to the Workshop before reloading or closing the tab.

The camp does not change your terrain map, its undo/redo history, ordinary open
run, campaign/Guild records, learned habits, Workshop shelf/draft, or expedition
checkpoint. Only an explicitly accepted Workshop handoff changes that draft.
World authoring edits game data in your browser; it cannot edit the published
game code or another player's world.
