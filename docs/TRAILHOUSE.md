# The Trailhouse

Choose **The Trailhouse** on the main menu, or **Workshop → Trailhouse**.
A village grows from the promises you keep on the road. There are no timers
outside a commission, purchases, accounts, or online requirements after the
website has loaded.

## Keeper commissions

Choose one of twelve favors and a challenge: **Trail** or **Nemesis**. Read
the keeper’s request before setting out. The ribbon above the map counts your
commission goals; open **Quest** for the individual goals. Restoring the world
and meeting the favor earns **three village light** the first time. Repeats
and the other rule set improve separate bests without awarding more light.
An ordinary world clear that misses a favor remains a clear, but does not earn
commission progress. You can leave, restart, or try another route freely.

## A village of your own

Spend earned light in **Your village**. Choose any restoration order; all
six projects cost **36 light** together, exactly twelve fulfilled commissions.
The Moth Post costs 3, the Listening Canal 5, the Bell Garden 6, the House of
Margins 6, the Little Observatory 7, and the Paper-Star Studio 9.

Each restoration changes the village illustration, unlocks a keepsake, and
opens two keeper conversation topics. **Remix their world** brings a copy into
the world studio; the normal draft-replacement confirmation protects your
existing work. Design your own alternate routes, test them, and share a world
or bind it into a Storybook using the existing creator tools.

Restore every place to unlock **A Night of Borrowed Lights**, a new original
festival adventure. Ring Home, Road, then Sky to open the pond crossing, light
three lanterns, and invite three old friends to the beacon. Four optional
invitations and a keeper conversation reward wandering. There are no wardens
at the celebration. Finish it for the seventh keepsake; replay it on either
rule set or remix your own festival after completing it.

## Keep your passport

Completed commissions, purchases, and bests save in this browser separately
from maps, Storybooks, campaign progress, and expeditions. An unfinished
attempt restarts after reloading. Opening the game never automatically starts
a commission.

Open **Your Trailhouse passport → Download passport** for a portable backup.
In another browser, choose **Restore passport**, review the incoming progress,
and confirm replacement of only the Trailhouse. Cancel leaves your current
village intact. Invalid files and files larger than 30 KB are rejected.
Browser saving failures leave the village usable for the current visit; download
a passport before closing the page. Localhost and public-site saves are separate.
