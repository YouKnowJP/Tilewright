# Eight new roads, two original stories

Open **Adventure Workshop → Storybooks**. The two **NEW STORY** covers appear
first. Each contains four original maps, opening scenes, keeper conversations,
chapter endings, and a final epilogue. Their worlds are also available in the
book creator's **World to add** list for your own adventures.

## The Rainmaker’s Letters

The town has waited an entire summer for rain. Postkeeper Nessa finds an
envelope addressed to the first rainy day. Carry its mystery through canals,
a listening orchard, and the coastal rain station. Nessa, Moss, and Ori each
remember a different part of the weather the town has lost.

| Chapter | Your journey | Something else to try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Undelivered Letter | Restore the roof lantern and deliver the letter. | Ask Nessa to open the south bridge, take supplies and work its wheel yourself, or use the northern key route. |
| The Two Sluices | Connect three banks and leave both wheels running. | Watch how each wheel also cools its crossing; gather the optional rain tokens on the lower bank. |
| The Bell Orchard | Ring Rain, Root, River, Home; collect all four seeds. | Find a shorter walking circuit. A wrong note resets the song; you can try again. |
| Where the Rain Comes Home | Restore two lamps and bring Nessa, Moss, and the rainseed home. | Recover the upper-road shell from its warden, or take the quieter low crossing. |

The orchard asks for walking rather than dashing. Coastal keepers join you only
after **both** lamps are restored. The rainseed must reach the home beacon;
picking it up is only the beginning of its journey.

## The Observatory of Borrowed Stars

An observatory has erased an apprentice from its ledger. Eid, a keeper's echo,
has waited a hundred years to finish a promise. Follow Astronomer Sable through
memory bridges and broken time to find Ilex—and decide how to give a sky back
without leaving anyone in the dark.

| Chapter | Your journey | Something else to try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Door That Remembers | Cross a three-part bridge and open the star archive. | Leave an Echoist double on the plate, or push the northern crate down for a lasting crossing and a no-power honor. |
| The Island Between Seconds | Travel three islands, ring Yesterday, Tomorrow, Now, and deliver the clock heart. | Carry the heart through paired waystones; walk between jumps to regain energy. |
| The Quiet Constellation | Restore two stars and rescue Ilex past an adaptive sentinel. | Follow the outer fern route quietly, or use mist, decoys, and shields to recover the guarded fragments. |
| A Star for Everyone | Hold two bridges, ring three bells, cool the far crossing, and bring Eid and the shared star home. | Leave permanent crate counterweights for the return trip; recover all three optional keepsakes. |

Walking on fern cover hides distant, quiet movement. A sentinel can still see
you at close range or remember a previous lead. Its committed outline shows
what it will do next. **Plan** and conversations spend no turns. For the guarded
treasure route, use the field pack and choose your moments; the clean outer
route does not need specialist powers.

## Read, replay, and make another version

Every chapter offers the existing optional promises. Missing a promise never
blocks the next page. Each new map has a verified clean route that meets its
speed target on both Trail and Nemesis. Alternate treasure and crate routes
may take longer, so choose the promise that fits your plan.

After a win, read the chapter ending and choose **Turn the page**. Open **Pages
you have restored**, then a chapter, to reread its opening and ending at any
later camp. Reading does not change your score or use a turn. Completed chapters
save at camp; reloading restarts only the open chapter.

Use **Make your version** to remix a whole storybook, or add any of the eight
worlds to a custom book. Chapters are independent snapshots. Downloads include
all their terrain, story, puzzle objects, and attached creator routes. See the
[Storybooks guide](STORYBOOKS.md) for sharing and checkpoint details.
