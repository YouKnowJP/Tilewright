# Lost Cartographer release verification — v0.29.0

## Delivered behavior

Six original chapters combine live rotating rooms, permanent crate weights,
reactive story cards, optional vault heists, and peaceful Two-Lantern cooperation.
All six are also shelf worlds with verified creator routes and Remix. Four tiny
hint puzzles teach the corresponding ideas in an independent run. Workshop
creation includes all four new object types, room size selection, bounded story
cards, authored clues, and portable version-2 adventures. Existing version-1
worlds and checkpoints retain their identities.

## Automated evidence

- **699 Node tests pass**, including 64 focused Cartographer checks and complete
  six-chapter Storybook runs on both difficulty settings.
- Every authored chapter and miniature has an executable clean witness. A second
  pair of witnesses opens all three vaults and escapes without damage on Trail
  and Nemesis. Ordinary victory still needs no vault.
- Pure-state tests freeze run snapshots while playing. Four rotations restore
  terrain and positions. Restart uses the initial definition. Handoff, story
  changes, and vault escalation survive Undo and exact checkpoint reconstruction.
- Actual checkpoints generated by v0.28 restore with matching fingerprints.
  Version-2 worlds round-trip through files, codes, nested Storybooks, and proofs.
- Invalid sizes, bounds, overlapping rooms, threat routes, cooperative hazards,
  missing story references, duplicate cards, oversized text, and invalid clues
  fail validation. Assemblies protect complete room boundaries and world-level
  story links. Legal object IDs cannot inherit rotation counts from prototypes.
- Tests cover a hunter entering a moving room, rings centered on a rotated vault,
  distinct cooperative roles, actor collision, carrying restrictions, and either
  traveler waiting at the beacon. Pathfinder receives a readable explanation
  when a light needs Lantern keeper.

## Limits of this evidence

WebKit is the tested engine, not a claim of testing every branded Safari version.
Touch viewports and controller polling are simulated; these checks do not replace
physical-device or human usability testing. This release provides shared-device
cooperation, not networking. Creator checks are structural and creator clears
prove their recorded route, not every possible puzzle state. Automated success
is not a guarantee of universal enjoyment or a promise that undiscovered bugs
cannot exist.
