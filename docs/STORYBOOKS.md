# Storybooks: a road between worlds

Choose **Choose your adventure → Storybooks**, or Adventure Workshop → Storybooks. Read a book's
opening scene before entering its first chapter. Thirteen featured books are available. Use the search field or choose New
adventures, Peaceful books, or More demanding books to narrow the shelves.
**The Rainmaker’s Letters** and **The Observatory of Borrowed Stars** each add
four original maps and a complete new story. The three earlier books weave the
six Puzzle Lab worlds into journeys of three, three, and six chapters.
See [the new story guide](NEW_STORIES.md) for all eight original levels.
**The Keeper’s Promise** adds five more original worlds across two three-chapter
roads, a real camp decision, and separate endings. Its [player and creator
guide](KEEPERS-PROMISE.md) also explains custom forward links and endings.

**The Welcome Lanterns** adds six peaceful chapters with no enemies or burning tiles. Use Accessibility → Find the peaceful storybook, or choose its cover in the library. See the [accessibility guide](ACCESSIBILITY.md) for reading sizes, map symbols, and controls.

**The Lanternfair Letters** adds four peaceful chapters about a missing pattern,
a ribbon crossing, a written bell song, and a shared parade. Its worlds also
appear in Discover and the book creator. Visit **Your traveler** to customize
your character and find the book. See the [traveler and festival guide](TRAVELER.md).

At each camp choose one optional promise:

| Promise | How to earn its honor |
| --- | --- |
| Follow the story | Relax and enjoy the chapter; no bonus honor. |
| Untouched light | Finish without taking damage. |
| A timely arrival | Finish within the displayed turn target. Nemesis has a tighter target. |
| Leave no glimmer | Find every treasure; offered when treasure is optional. |
| Every voice matters | Speak to every guide; required rescue keepers are separate. |
| An ordinary kind of magic | Finish without using specialist powers; try another puzzle solution. |

An earned honor adds **150 points**. Missing it never blocks the story. Some
player-made worlds need their specialist power, so choose another promise if
that world's puzzle requires it. The gameplay ribbon tracks your chosen goal;
**Turn the page** returns to camp after a win. Completed chapter receipts and
full-book bests are separate from ordinary Workshop results and creator clears.
Full-book bests favor honors, then points, then fewer turns, separately for
each book version and Trail/Nemesis rules. **Pages you have restored** also
lets you open a completed chapter to reread its opening scene and ending.

## Bind your own journey

Select **Bind your own story** or **Make your version** on a featured book.
Write a title, creator name, invitation, and ending. Add **two to six worlds**
from your saved shelf, featured adventures, Puzzle Lab, the fifty original
Storybook levels, or current World studio
draft. Add a short scene before each chapter, move chapters earlier or later,
and remove any you no longer need. A world must pass structural checks before
it can join a book; a completed playtest is still the reliable way to prove its
puzzles can be solved.

Each chapter is an independent snapshot. Editing its original world later
does not change an existing book. To update a chapter, remove it and add the
updated original. Repeated worlds are allowed: give a return visit a new scene
and try a different optional challenge.

The Clockwork Post adds three peaceful programmable-companion chapters.

There is one saved book draft per journey. Each book version/rule combination
keeps a chapter and branch bookmark (up to 32, without silent eviction).
Starting another book preserves the earlier bookmark;
replacing a populated book draft also asks first. The World studio draft,
saved world shelf, campaign, training, Guild, and expeditions remain separate.

## Share and resume

**Download storybook** creates a `.storybook.json` file containing every
chapter's terrain, objects, story, and any attached creator clear. Send the
file using your usual messaging app. A friend opens the same public game and
chooses **Workshop → Storybooks → Import storybook**. They can review or edit
the imported book before playing it. No repository access or account is needed.
Files contain game data and text, with no scripts or external artwork.

Up to six chapters fit in a **1.6 MB** book; each world retains its existing
400 KB limit. The file includes the book, **not play progress**. Download as
many books as you want, then import them when you want to revisit one.

Completed chapter progress saves **at camp**. The latest open adventure also
keeps its exact board, supplies, hunter habits, and Undo history across reloads.
Use **Main menu → Continue trail** or **Resume chapter** to return. Starting a
different adventure replaces this latest-trail slot; the book still keeps its
completed chapters and selected promise, and offers **Restart chapter** if its
exact run is no longer available. Every new chapter starts with fresh supplies
and hunter habits; a retry starts that chapter over. Browsing another book's
camp preserves the latest exact Continue slot until you start a different
adventure. Returning through Continue trail also reopens the correct book.
Failed browser saving is reported, and books remain playable for the visit.
Keep the tab open to retain session-only progress, and download any book you
want to keep before clearing browser data.

Sharing is by portable file; this release does not provide an online gallery,
cloud saves, or automatic transfer between devices.

Branching books use format v2 with optional forward chapter links, route choices,
and route-specific endings. Linear v1 books retain their existing identity and
save compatibility. See [the branching creator guide](KEEPERS-PROMISE.md#write-branching-adventures).


## Three new long roads — v0.30

**The Weatherkeepers’ Almanac**, **The Night Post**, and **The Open Workshop**
add eighteen original worlds. The first explores crossings, songs, rotating
glass, and a rainseed return; the second adds longer courier routes and clearly
optional treasures; the third is peaceful cooperation on one device. Every
chapter supports Trail/Nemesis, chapter promises, exact Continue, Undo, route
review, and remixing. That release brought the library to nine original storybooks with
47 unique levels, plus three journeys assembled from Puzzle Lab worlds.
See [the chapter list](SCREEN_AND_SAGAS.md).

Bookmarks keep completed chapters and choices, not every suspended board. Only the latest Continue slot retains an exact in-chapter position. Use a complete game backup to keep bookmarks; a downloaded Storybook contains its worlds, not your progress. Your story bookmarks lists older custom books too. Removing an inactive bookmark asks for confirmation. If storage is full or blocked, keep the tab open; unsaved bookmark changes last only for the visit.
